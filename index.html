Hi, I’m Izzy Sunby

Welcome! I am a first-year PhD Student in Harvard's Department of Government, where I study International Relations, Comparative Politics, and Quantitative Methods. My research interests lie in autocratic states' foreign policy strategies in international organizations & the international political economy and the domestic political effects of international cooperation with a substantive focus on Latin America and East Asia. Outside of research, I love to learn foreign languages, read fiction, cook new foods, explore local coffee shops, and spend time with my two black cats, Coal and Lala. I hold a Bachelor of Science with Comprehensive Honors in Political Science and Spanish with Certificates in Chinese Professional Communication and Statistics from the University of Wisconsin-Madison.
